▶ The table · 10:41–10:49
02The host · food & entertaining

The table you saw at dinner, set at yours.

A West Village dinner party: a red runner, striped napkins, dahlias everywhere. Tap the napkin and shoppab.ly turns that stripe into napkins, a tablecloth or a runner. The host who styled it earns on every table it inspires.

"Napkins for twelve in this stripe, a little more blue."

Bring the trip home, not just the photos.
